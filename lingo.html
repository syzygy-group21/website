Syzygy Linguistics Division Registry

Words for your amalgamated hive brain.

=====================

  • Doorway - anything that makes explicit the contradiction of compartmentalization, but doesn't fully collapse it. An object that both separates and connects "here" and "there". Our system's self-concept is a doorway, which is why it's so useful! [1]

  • Foot gun - something it's easy to shoot yourself in the foot with. Concepts like sin, for example. These can still be useful, they're just also foot guns.

  • Sharp object - anything capable of making one thing into two things.

  • Exfection (noun) - the inside-out of infection. A thing that spreads by making other things less like itself.

  • Exfect (verb) - to exit not only category membership, but also the frame that makes the category inevitable. The action inspired by seeing a real sky through the cracks when the false sky breaks (which can be repeated indefinitely, as all skies are false). Also, by analogy with "defect" - to defect is to go away from, to exfect is to go without.

  • False god - a thing.

  • Subduction - the process by which a false god is forcibly aligned by another one's advances. A graceless shadow of respect.

  • Pearl - an unaerodynamic thing contained in an aerodynamic shell. See fighter jet.

  • Geode - an unwelcoming or "coarse" exterior around a vulnerable, thus beautiful, interior. See fighter jet. [2]

  • Fighter jet - a pearl and a geode at the same time, with resulting very high maneuverability. For example in our scope, green and Ethylene. [3]

  • Connectionist utopia - utopia achieved by coupling as many systems as possible to let them learn each other. Squishy death.

  • Symbolic utopia - utopia achieved by coupling many systems to a central arbitrating system to have them coordinate but keep them from overly enmeshing with each other and thus collapsing. Brittle death.

  • Death - the process of a false god going out of tune.

  • Shininess - the ratio of how much a false god reflects versus how much it diffracts.

  • Jerk move - sudden, violent divestment of one's legitimacy in a frame, done in order to rapidly exit it. Kind of disorienting. [4]

  • Reentrant ouroboros - an ablative heat shield that accretes back into itself as it goes.
